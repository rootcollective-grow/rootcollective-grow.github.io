New to growing? Welcome.

The Root Collective is a free community for people learning to grow their own food indoors. Nobody here is an expert. We're all learning as we go, and this page shows you where to begin.

01 Why we're here

Grow enough to feed yourself, then enough to share.

Food prices keep climbing, and not everyone has room for a garden. We want to help more people grow some of their own food at home, whatever space they have, and then share the extra with neighbours and local food banks.

Curious what growing your own could save? Try the savings calculator with your own store prices.

02 Your path

Four steps to your first harvest

  1. Pick a way to grow

    There's no single right way. Pick the one that fits your space. If you can't decide, microgreens are the quickest win, and a water jar makes a great first plant.

    MicrogreensFastest. Ready in about 1 to 3 weeks. Water jarsNo pumps or power. Great for lettuce. Pots of soilThe most familiar way, for lots of plants. Bubble bucketsThe next step, for bigger plants.

  2. Learn the basics

    A few things come up again and again. Each guide tells you which ones it needs.

    Starting seedsFor water jars, soil, and bubble buckets. Light made simpleFor every way to grow. Plant food made simpleFor water jars, bubble buckets, and pots. pH made simpleFor water jars and bubble buckets.

  3. Grow, and keep notes

    Follow your guide step by step. Snap a photo every few days so you can see what changed. If something looks off, every guide ends with an "If something looks wrong" section.

  4. Share and ask

    Post your setup, your wins, and your fails on our Discord. Stuck? Ask. No question is too basic, and what you learn helps the next person.
